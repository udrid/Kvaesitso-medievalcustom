Breaking the seal

S7 Part 1, preview only. Welcome animation when Home appears after you unlock the phone. The real lock screen stays Samsung's. Shown at the real size, 411 x 914 dp, in Gold and Oxblood and in Ember. Nothing here is built yet.

1. How the launcher knows "Home appears right after an unlock"

All of these need no new permission. All status below is UNVERIFIED on the phone (written from Android documentation and known behaviour; to be confirmed with a small probe on the S20 FE).

ACTION_USER_PRESENT (receiver registered only while Home is resumed or waiting)The user finished unlocking (PIN, fingerprint, face, swipe).Works for a runtime-registered receiver without a permission. Known quirks: not sent by every OEM for every unlock type, so it is a second opinion, not the only one.

Recommended recipe (reliable enough, small): keep the existing "Home paused while the screen was off" flag. When Home resumes with that flag: if isKeyguardLocked is false, arm and play after ~150 ms; if true, wait for USER_PRESENT (receiver only while resumed, removed at pause), with a 3 s timeout that gives up silently. Consume the flag when it plays: a later Home press while the phone is unlocked never plays. Pressing Home from another app is not an unlock, so nothing plays.

Limits that stay: (a) it cannot draw on the lock screen itself, only on Home once Home is under your finger; (b) if you locked the phone while inside another app (not Home), you unlock back into that app and there is no welcome; (c) with fingerprint unlock Samsung's own unlock animation runs first, so the welcome starts as that ends (about 0.2 to 0.4 s later); (d) Always-on display wakes and notification peeks are not unlocks.

2. Three variants (about 1.5 s, once)

Key frames are the real animation sampled at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent of the time. Under each picture: the time in milliseconds. All three: the screen takes touches from frame 0 (the animation is a non-blocking picture on top, nothing waits), gate closed = Home simply appears, Home ends in exactly its normal look. Sparks are tiny.

4. What every variant shares (rules obeyed)

  1. Plays only when the motion gate is open: Home resumed, screen on, Battery Saver off, battery above 15 % or charging, Android's remove-animations off, Animations setting not Off. Gate closed = Home just appears.
  2. Plays at most once per unlock, never on a plain Home press, never after the app comes back from another app.
  3. Never delays touch: the overlay does not take touches; the orb, dock and shields work from the first frame. Any tap ends the animation at once (jump to the final look).
  4. New setting Welcome animation: On / Off, right under Animations. Default On. When Animations is Off the welcome is off too.
  5. Sparks and glow only as small regions; no looping; always ends in the normal Home look; extra pictures use the current palette (Gold or Ember shown; Verdigris, Bone, Royal follow the same colour roles).
  6. No sound, no vibration (could be a later choice).

5. NOT verified

  • Nothing has run on the phone. Which unlock signals fire on One UI 5 with your lock type (fingerprint, face, PIN, none) is UNVERIFIED: a one-evening probe screen can settle it after the design is picked.
  • The timing (feel) and how it sits next to Samsung's own unlock animation can only be judged on the phone.
  • This preview is a browser drawing with a simplified Home; real frame rate, battery use and the seal pictures are estimates.
  • Whether the 1.5 s feels too long after the hundredth unlock: that is why there is an Off switch, and a possible "play only the first unlock of each hour" option (question 5 below).
