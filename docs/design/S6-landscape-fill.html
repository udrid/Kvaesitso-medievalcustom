Landscape Home: filling the empty space

Design preview S6 for the Deliverance launcher. Nothing here is built. Pictures are drawn at 844 x 390; your S20 FE is 914 x 411 dp, so on the phone everything is about 9% bigger than drawn. Sample values (63°, event names) are only there to show the look. [M] marks a part that could be sold (see ops/monetization.md).

1. What the screen looks like today (measured)

What the numbers say

    So: scaling the stage up does almost nothing, because it already fills the free area (99%). The empty space is inside the layout: the columns are laid out for a stage 760 wide and 374 high, but the content only uses about two thirds of it. The three biggest holes are the bottom of the right column, the bottom of the left column and the strip between the shields and the dock. To fill them the content has to be taller, or something new has to move in.

    Today (baseline), with the holes marked

    Red hatching = empty. Camera on the LEFT (buttons on the right).

    Portrait: stays exactly as it is

    Portrait is not touched by any option. It is shown so you can see how the same sections look when they have room.

    Option A: scale up to the whole free area

    1. The stage is matched to the free area exactly (today it is 99% already) and the gutters between the columns shrink from 10 to 0. The side columns grow from 222 to 232 wide, so left and right content is about 4.5% bigger.
    2. The orb zone cannot grow: its four shields already span the whole 296 middle column.

    Good: small and safe. Not good: the holes stay, about 30% of the stage is still empty, so on its own it does not answer your feedback. Cost: small (stage numbers and column width in MedievalLandscape.kt; no new code).

    Option B: taller columns, more rows

    1. Ledger shows 4 events (today 2). Dispatches shows 5 rows (today 3). Both panels grow downward.
    2. Treasury is drawn 8% bigger (text and coin); the four pennants 25% bigger, with a bigger value number.
    3. Orb zone moves down a little and the dock sits lower, closing most of the gap between shields and dock. The orb keeps its size and stays the hero.
    4. Right column about 130 taller, left about 20 taller. A hole of about 60 stays at the bottom of the left column and 50 in the middle.

    Good: no new data, no new permission, no new battery use: the same data in more rows. Cost: medium: the Ledger and Dispatches row counts must follow the height (today fixed at 2 and 3), and Treasury and the pennants need a "tall" version.

    Option D: A + B + one extra panel (recommended)

    1. Right column as in B but 4 rows of Dispatches: Ledger with 4 events, command bar, Dispatches with 4 rows, drawn 4.5% bigger (232 wide, as A).
    2. Left column as in B: bigger Treasury and bigger pennants, 232 wide.
    3. Middle: the orb stays the hero at the same size and goes slightly up; the new Forecast strip (six hours) sits in the gap between the shields and the dock; the dock stays at the bottom.
    4. Camera and button clearances, the pill, palette colours and the 350 ms turn glide are unchanged. The strip is drawn once and changes only when the weather does (no always-running animation).

    Good: every column ends near the bottom, one new piece of information, the orb is not squeezed. About 60 stays empty at the bottom of the left column. Cost: largest: B's work plus one new panel (data already there). After a build, check the small text on the phone.
