Animation menu (tick to try)

Proposed setting "Animations":
Full = everything ticked (at most two ambient at once). Minimal (default) = 1. flame flicker plus 4 and 5 only. Off = nothing moves.
Rules that apply to all of it: nothing moves unless Home is on screen; nothing moves in Battery Saver, at 15% battery or less (unless charging), or if Android's "Remove animations" is on; ambient effects at most 12 per second; at most two ambient at once.
Honest estimate (not measured): with the flame alone the screen does about 10 small redraws a second, on the order of 1% extra battery per hour of screen-on Home time. Android's battery usage list for Deliverance will show the true figure after a few days.
