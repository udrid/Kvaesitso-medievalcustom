# C5 spec (approved by the owner)

Look at `C5.png`, then read `C5.html` for exact colors, sizes, spacing and SVG shapes (390x844). Style: Kingdom Come: Deliverance, dark: charcoal vellum, aged gold, oxblood. Fonts: UnifrakturCook (headings), Cinzel (labels), IM Fell English (body). Film grain and dark vignette over everything.

Top to bottom:
1. Camera pill around the punch-hole: "Ready" (Claude status) left, battery % right. Needs its own overlay service and permission; build last.
2. Header: red blackletter "S" drop cap plus "unday", "25th of October", big clock, weather line.
3. Status line: Wi-Fi call, Bluetooth, Hushed (mute), voicemail count.
4. Treasury panel (red ribbon tab): gold coin, total, goal bar, "of the way". Placeholders only.
5. Four swallow-tail pennants: Vigor (battery), Stores (storage), Wits (memory), Heat (temperature), gold fill rises with the value.
6. Center orb (Claude): tap opens the Claude app. Ring text: "VERITAS ET CONSILIUM + ANNO DOMINI + ORACULUM". Label "The Oracle".
7. Four heater shields around the orb: Twitter, Discord (badge), Videos, Messages (badge).
8. Today's Ledger: next two calendar events.
9. Command bar "Command the Oracle..." with SUMMON.
10. Dispatches: notification list with "N unheeded".
11. Dock: four oxblood wax seals: Herald (Phone), Portrait (Camera), Road (Web), Smithy (Settings).

Values in [brackets] are placeholders. The badge counts 3 and 2 are samples.
Not yet confirmed by the owner: dock names, the Latin motto, what "Command the Oracle" does when tapped, the app name.
