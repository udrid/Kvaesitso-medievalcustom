# T23 test list: "Recently used" panel on landscape Home

1. Hold the phone upright (portrait) on Home. You should see the usual Home. Nothing new at the bottom-left. Report if anything changed.
2. Turn the phone sideways. Under the four flags (VIGOR, STORES, WITS, HEAT) you should see a small panel with a red ribbon "RECENTLY USED". If you have not opened any app yet it says "Open an app and it will appear here".
3. Open three apps one after another from this launcher: tap a shield, tap a dock seal, tap a result from the typing layer. Come back to Home each time (swipe up / Home).
4. Turn the phone sideways again. The three apps should appear as small gold shields with names, the one you opened last on the left.
5. Open the first app again. It should jump to the left and appear only once.
6. Tap one of the small shields. The app should open (a short gold flash first). Report which one.
7. Turn the phone the other way round (camera on the other side). The panel should still sit under the flags, clear of the camera pill and the navigation bar.
8. Close the launcher fully and open it again, turn sideways: the same apps should still be there.

Should NOT happen:
- Portrait Home looks different.
- The panel covers the flags or the camera pill, or touches the navigation bar.
- An app is listed twice.
- An app you opened only from the Samsung app drawer or another launcher is listed (only this launcher's own taps count).
