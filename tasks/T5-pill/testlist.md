# Owner checks on the phone: T5-pill

Before you start: install the new APK. The notification icons need "notification access": if the Dispatches panel already shows your notifications, it is on.

1. Open the launcher (press Home). Look at the very top of the screen.
   - Samsung's own top bar (clock, signal, battery) should be gone. A small black pill with a thin gold edge should sit around the camera hole, with the hole in its middle.
   - Report: is the camera hole centred in the pill, and is the pill in front of (not behind) the camera?
2. Look at the right side of the pill. You should see the Wi-Fi icon (if you are on Wi-Fi) and signal bars.
   - Turn Wi-Fi off in the quick panel: the Wi-Fi icon disappears and the pill gets slightly shorter. Turn it on again.
   - Report how many signal bars you see and whether they match what you had before.
3. Turn Bluetooth on, then off (pull down the panel from the top, tap the Bluetooth tile).
   - A Bluetooth icon appears when on and disappears when off, within a second or two.
4. Hushed: set the phone to silent or vibrate (volume button, tap the sound icon), or turn on Do Not Disturb.
   - A small moon appears on the right. Set the sound back to normal: it disappears.
5. Notifications: ask someone to message you, or send yourself one. Icons of the apps that sent them (up to 4, then "+2" and so on) appear on the left of the pill. Dismiss them in Dispatches: the icons go away and the pill gets shorter.
6. Pull down from the very top edge of the screen: the normal quick panel must still open.
7. Should NOT happen:
   - The pill covering or sitting off to the side of the camera hole.
   - The top bar still showing on Home, or the header moving down or up compared with before.
   - Taps just under the pill (for example on the header) not working.
   - The top bar missing on other screens (open the search by swiping up, open Settings from the header long-press, open any other app): report it if it is.
8. Restart check: swipe the launcher away from recent apps and open it again. The pill and its icons should come back right away.

Report back: what you saw at each step, and a photo of the top of the screen if something looks off.
