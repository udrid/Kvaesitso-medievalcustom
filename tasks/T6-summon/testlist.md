# Owner checks on the phone: T6-summon

Install the new APK from the GitHub Actions run for branch `claude/T6-summon` (it installs over the old Deliverance). The Claude app must be installed and signed in.

## A. SUMMON on the Home screen
1. Press the Home button. Look at the long bar "Command the Oracle…".
   You should see: a small dark-red button with the gold word SUMMON at its right end.
2. Tap SUMMON without typing anything.
   You should see: a small message "Write thy question first." and nothing else happens.
   You should NOT see: the Claude app open.

## B. Typing a question, then SUMMON (Claude app closed)
1. Close the Claude app completely (Android recent apps button, swipe the Claude card away).
2. On Home tap the bar "Command the Oracle…". The search opens with the keyboard.
   Type: what is the capital of Peru
   You should see: the SUMMON button appears inside the search box, next to the little filter icon, once you typed something.
3. Tap SUMMON.
   You should see: the Claude app opens, and a message "Copied. Paste if needed." appears briefly.
   Report: (a) did the Claude app open? (b) is it a NEW empty chat with your words in the message box? (c) are the words in the box and NOT sent yet?
4. If the words are NOT in the box: touch the box for a second, tap Paste. Report that the words appeared.
5. Tap Send inside the Claude app yourself.

## C. Same, with an OLD chat open
1. Open the Claude app, open any old conversation, then press Home.
2. Do B2 and B3 again with: tell me a joke
   Report: (b) did you land in a NEW chat, or was "tell me a joke" added to the old conversation's box?

## D. Should NOT happen
1. Your words must NOT be lost: after SUMMON, long-press any text box and Paste. Your question must appear.
2. The Claude app must NOT send the question by itself.
3. Search must work as before: type "calc" or an app name in the search; results appear, tapping a result opens it, the clear (X) button empties the box. SUMMON must NOT change any of this.
4. With the search box empty, there must be NO SUMMON button inside the search.

## E. Restart check
Nothing is saved by SUMMON, so there is nothing to restart-check. Optional: force stop Deliverance (Settings > Apps > Deliverance > Force stop), press Home, and confirm the SUMMON button is still on the bar.
