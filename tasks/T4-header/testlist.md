# Owner checks on the phone: T4-header

Install the new APK from the GitHub Actions run for branch `claude/T4-header` (same way as before; it installs over the old Deliverance).
Compare with the pictures `docs/design/T4-header.png` and `docs/design/T4-weather-A.png`.

## A. The new header (today)
1. Press the Home button so you see the Deliverance home screen.
   You should see at the top: a small gold line "DAY OF SOL" (or today's planet), then a big red painted first letter with the rest of the weekday in cream gothic letters, and the date under it.
2. Look to the right of the weekday name.
   You should see: a round gold medallion with today's planet sign, and at the far right a small time, the weather line under it, and a tiny moving landscape (hills, a tree, a castle with a red flag).
3. Look under the header.
   You should see: a gold ornamented divider line, then "Bluetooth" and "Hushed".
   Report: does any text overlap the medallion or get cut off with "..."? (A long weather name like "Partly cloudy" may end in "...".)

## B. The weather picture
1. Watch the little landscape for 5 seconds.
   You should see: it moves in small jumps (the flag flaps; sun rays twitch, rain falls, or stars twinkle, depending on the weather). It should NOT glide smoothly.
2. Compare it with the weather words above it.
   You should see: a matching scene (clear = sun with a face, cloudy = clouds, rain = blue rain, storm = yellow lightning, snow = flakes, fog = mist bands; at night with a clear sky = moon and stars).
   Report: the weather words and what the picture shows.
3. If it is a clear night: look at the moon.
   You should see: the same shape as the real moon tonight (full, half, crescent...).

## C. All seven days (uses the phone's date setting; put it back afterwards)
1. Open Settings > General management > Date and time. Turn OFF "Automatic date and time".
2. Tap "Set date", pick tomorrow, tap Done. Press Home.
   You should see: a different planet line, letter, medallion and divider (Monday = Luna, Tuesday = Mars, Wednesday = Mercury, Thursday = Jupiter, Friday = Venus, Saturday = Saturn, Sunday = Sol). It can take up to 5 seconds to change.
3. Repeat step 2 for each of the 7 days. Then turn "Automatic date and time" back ON.
   Report: any day whose name, letter or medallion looks wrong, overlaps, or is cut off (Wednesday is the longest).

## D. Everything else is a bit bigger
1. Look at Treasury, the four pennants, the shields, the orb, the panels and the dock.
   You should see: all of them about 8% larger than before, nothing cut off at the left or right edge, the dock labels (HERALD ... SMITHY) fully visible above the Android buttons.
2. Tap a shield, the orb, the command bar and one dock seal.
   You should see: each opens what it opened before (taps land where you press).
3. Swipe a dispatch left or right.
   You should see: it still dismisses like before.

## D2. The ring around the Oracle
1. Look at the gold ring around the orb in the middle of the home screen.
   You should see: three phrases, NOSCE TE IPSUM at the top, SAPERE AUDE at the lower right, LUX ET VERITAS at the lower left, in bold capitals. Together they go almost all the way round, with a small gold cross between each pair.
2. Turn your head, don't turn the phone: every letter should stand upright and be easy to read (the two lower phrases read left to right along the bottom).
3. Look at the two thin ring lines.
   You should see: the letters sit in the middle between them and touch neither line.
   Report: any letter touching a ring line, letters overlapping each other, or a phrase with big uneven gaps.

## E. Things that should NOT happen
1. The weather picture should NOT show when the weather line says "[weather]" (no weather set up); that spot stays empty.
2. The phone should NOT get warm or the battery drain faster than before while the home screen is open.
3. Nothing in the header should flicker or jump position while the picture moves.

## F. Restart check
1. Swipe Deliverance away from the recent-apps list, then press Home to open it again.
   You should see: the same header, medallion and weather picture as before. Your shield picks and Treasury are unchanged.

## G. Small screen (if you can)
1. Settings > Display > Screen zoom: move it one step bigger. Press Home.
   You should see: the whole page shrinks to fit, with no overlaps. Then set Screen zoom back.
