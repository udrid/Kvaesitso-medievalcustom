# T21 phone checks: always-on display pictures (plain words)

1. Open the launcher's Settings (long-press Home or the Settings shield). Near the top, under "Welcome animation", you should see "Always-on display pictures". Tap it.
   You should see: a framed picture of today's weekday, a gold button "Save today's picture to Gallery", "Save all eight", and a 5-step card.
2. Tap "Save today's picture to Gallery".
   You should see the line "Saved to Pictures/Deliverance". Tap it again: "Already in Pictures/Deliverance".
3. Open the Gallery app. Open Albums. You should see an album "Deliverance" with the picture (black, a gold ring, the day name).
4. Back in the launcher page, tap "Save all eight". The album should now hold eight pictures.
5. Tap "Open Always On Display settings". Samsung's Display settings open (or the main Settings). Follow the 5 steps on the page: Lock screen, Always On Display, Clock style, Image clock, Gallery, pick the Deliverance picture, crop, Done.
   Report: which screen opened, and whether you found Always On Display from there.
6. Lock the phone and look at the always-on display. Report how the picture is cropped (is the ring cut off?).
7. Restart the launcher (swipe it away in Recents, open it again) and repeat step 2: it should say "Already in...", the album must not get duplicates.

Should NOT happen: a permission prompt, a crash, an empty or missing Deliverance album, duplicates of the same picture, the picture changing by itself next day.
