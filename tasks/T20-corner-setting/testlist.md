# Phone checks for T20: Corner style
1. Open the launcher. Tap the weekday on the home screen to open Settings, then tap "Appearance".
   You should see only two entries: "Transparency" and "Corner style" (no Theme import/export, no Colour source). Report if you see more.
2. Tap "Corner style". It should say "Sharp: square corners on buttons and chips" at first. Choose "Round", then go back.
3. Go Home. Tap the command bar ("Command the Oracle") so the typing layer opens. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Flashlight, Auto-rotate, Power saving, Location and Sound should now be round pills. Without closing the launcher, repeat step 2 with "Sharp": they should turn square.
4. Open any dialog (long-press an app or search result, choose hide). "Cancel" and "Hide" should be round in Round and square in Sharp.
5. Should NOT happen: panels, cards, frames or ribbons turning round; the wax-seal switches (in Settings) turning square; the change needing a restart or rotation.
6. Choose "Round", swipe the launcher away from recent apps, open it again. Appearance should still say Round.
