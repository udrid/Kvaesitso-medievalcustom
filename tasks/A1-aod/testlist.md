# A1-aod: owner steps (always-on display pictures)

What you get: eight small dim-gold pictures (Sunday to Saturday, plus one "plain" ring) shaped for the little picture frame Samsung shows under its clock on the always-on screen. Picture to look at first: `docs/design/A1-aod.png`.

Note: the pictures live on a branch that is NOT pushed yet. The coordinator will tell you when it is and give the branch name. Below, BRANCH means that name.

## 1. Get the pictures onto the phone
1. On the phone, open the browser and go to https://github.com/udrid/Kvaesitso-medievalcustom
2. Tap the branch button (it says "main"), pick BRANCH, then open the folders `assets`, `aod`, `imageclock`.
3. Tap one file, tap the download icon, and repeat for the days you want. You should see them in the Gallery under Downloads. (Other ways: Quick Share from the PC, or email the files to yourself.)

## 2. Put one into the always-on display
1. Open Settings, tap Lock screen, tap Always On Display, make sure it is switched on, tap Clock style.
2. Tap the "Image clock" tab, tap Gallery, pick today's picture (Sunday = Sol, Monday = Luna, Tuesday = Mars, Wednesday = Mercury, Thursday = Jupiter, Friday = Venus, Saturday = Saturn; "plain" works any day).
3. Drag the crop so the whole picture fills the frame, pick a clock colour (gold-ish or white), tap Done. You should see the clock with your picture under it.

## 3. Check it
1. Press the power button to lock the phone, then tap the screen or leave it so the always-on display shows.
2. You should see Samsung's clock and date, and under it a dim gold medallion with the day name, on black.
3. Report: does the picture show? Is anything cut off at the edges or corners? Is the clock easy to read? Is the picture too bright, or too dark to see?

## Should NOT happen
- A bright box, a grey frame or a lit-up rectangle around the picture (the background must be black).
- Any large bright patch (it could burn into the screen over time).
- The picture moving, or the medallion cut off at the rounded corners.

## Change the picture each day, by hand
Repeat step 2 each morning (it takes under a minute). Automatic daily change needs Good Lock, which is not available on this phone, so there is no way to do it for you.

## Notes
- Still pictures only. A GIF would keep the screen working and cost battery, so none are provided.
- The files are 1200 x 900 (4:3) and all dim gold on pure black. Black pixels on this screen are switched off, which saves battery.
