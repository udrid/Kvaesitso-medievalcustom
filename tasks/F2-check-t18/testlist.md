# Phone test: starred contacts light up (T18, checked by F2)

You need one friend who is in your Contacts and can text and call you. Keep Smart Launcher installed as a backup.

**Visible result:** in Deliverance's typing layer, your friend's round tile gets a small red badge with a number when they texted or called and you have not looked yet.

1. Star your friend.
   - Open the Samsung **Contacts** app and tap your friend's name.
   - Tap the **star** (Favourite) so it turns yellow. Go back.
2. Open the typing layer and look.
   - Press Home. Tap the bar at the bottom ("Command the Oracle").
   - You should see your friend's round tile in "People and switches", with NO badge yet.
3. A message arrives while you watch.
   - Keep the typing layer open. Ask your friend to send you one text (SMS).
   - You should see a red "1" appear on their tile, and a soft gold glow ring the tile once (about 2 seconds).
4. A second message.
   - Ask for one more text, or a WhatsApp message if your friend uses WhatsApp.
   - The number goes up (2), the badge swells once, the glow plays once more.
5. A missed call.
   - Ask your friend to call you. Do not answer; let it ring out.
   - Open the typing layer. The number should go up by one.
6. Tap the tile with the badge.
   - Tap your friend's tile.
   - The message app (or phone app) should open on that conversation, NOT the contact card.
7. Clear it.
   - Pull down the notification shade and swipe all of your friend's notifications away.
   - Open the typing layer again: the badge is gone. Tapping the tile now opens the contact card as before.
8. Restart check.
   - Ask for one more text. Then open recent apps (the button left of Home) and swipe Deliverance away.
   - Press Home, open the typing layer: the badge "1" is back. Clear the notification afterwards.

## Should NOT happen
- A badge on anyone else's tile (another starred friend who did not write).
- A badge staying after you swiped the notification away.
- A badge from an e-mail, a calendar reminder or an app update.
- With Battery Saver on (pull down the shade, tap **Power saving**): a new message gives the badge but NO glow and NO swell. Turn Power saving off afterwards.
- In a group chat (if you have one with this friend): a message from SOMEONE ELSE in the group must not light your friend's tile.

## Please report
- For each step: did you see what it says? Yes / No.
- Step 5 (missed call): badge yes or no. This is the one most likely to fail on Samsung.
- Which apps you tried (Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, Phone) and whether each gave a badge.
- A screenshot (press Power + Volume down) if something looks wrong.
