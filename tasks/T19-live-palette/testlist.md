# Phone test: T19-live-palette
1. Go to the Home screen. Press and hold the weekday (the big letter at the top left) to open Settings.
   You should see the launcher settings.
2. Choose the colour scheme setting and pick a different one, for example Royal Blue. Press Back until you are on Home.
   You should see at once: the big letter, weekday, date, the round planet badge, the divider line and the little weather picture all in the new colour. Do NOT turn the phone.
3. Repeat with Ember and Bone. Then pick Gold and Oxblood again.
   You should see the header change each time, like the rest of the screen.
4. Close the launcher fully (swipe it away in recent apps) and open it again.
   You should see the last scheme you picked, header included.
Should NOT happen: any gold left in the header in a blue scheme (or blue left in a gold one); the header only changing after you turn the phone.
Report: which scheme, and which header part kept the old colour.
