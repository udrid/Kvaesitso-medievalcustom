# Test list: S4 colour scheme (do this on the phone after the APK is installed)

1. Open Deliverance. Home should look exactly as before (gold and oxblood). Report any colour that looks different.
2. Swipe to open the launcher settings (the first screen of Settings). At the top you should see a row "Colour scheme" above "Animations", showing "Gold and Oxblood".
3. Tap "Colour scheme". A list of five names appears: Gold and Oxblood, Ember, Verdigris, Bone, Royal Blue and Silver. Tap "Ember", then go back to Home.
4. Home should now be amber: the ribbons deep red-brown, text and frames amber-gold, the glass orb glowing amber. Check: the weekday letter, clock, weather picture, the four pennants, shields, the orb and ring, the four wax seals at the bottom. Report anything still in the old gold.
5. Repeat step 3 and 4 for Verdigris (green), Bone (grey-white with red ribbons) and Royal Blue and Silver (blue). For each, tell me: is the orb picture right (no grey box, no square edges), is all small text readable?
6. Open the typing layer (tap the command bar) and the app picker with each colour. They should follow the colour too.
7. Should NOT happen: Home must not flicker, jump, or change layout when you switch; the orb must not go black or show a coloured square; nothing should be pure white.
8. Restart test: pick Royal Blue and Silver, then close the launcher completely (swipe it away from the recent apps) and open it again. It should still be blue. Then set it back to Gold and Oxblood.
9. Tell me which screens still show the old look (expected: the settings screens, search results and other non-Home screens until the S2 work is merged).
