# Phone test: T16-polish

1. Divider and weather (all seven days)
   - Be on Home. Look at the line of little ornaments under the day name and clock.
   - You should see: the line runs across the left and middle and ends a little before the weather picture on the right. Nothing touches or crosses the weather picture.
   - To see the other days: Settings > General management > Date and time > turn off "Automatic date and time", set the date to each weekday in turn (Sunday to Saturday), go back to Home. Each day has its own ornaments.
   - Report: any day where the line or an ornament touches the weather picture (tell me the day).
2. Gap
   - You should see: the Treasury panel sits a little closer to the line under the header than before. The Dispatches panel is a little taller. The four dock seals are exactly where they were.
3. Contacts in the typing layer
   - Tap the Command bar. Without typing, look at the framed panel above the switches.
   - If it says "Tap to allow contacts": tap that line, then tap Allow on the phone's question. The line should change.
   - You should see either your starred contacts, or the line "Star contacts in your Contacts app to see them here" (then star one contact in the Contacts app, come back, and it should appear).
   - With the keyboard open, scroll the layer with your finger: the most used apps are at the top, the rest scrolls.
4. Stars
   - Watch the orb for about 30 seconds. The gold stars should wink rarely and softly.
   - Report: better / still too much / too dead.
5. Should NOT happen
   - The line or any ornament touching or crossing the weather picture.
   - Any panel (Treasury, shields, ledger, command bar, dock) changing size. Only Dispatches grew.
   - The dock, the camera pill or the header moving.
   - A contacts question appearing without you tapping the line.
6. Restart
   - Force-stop the launcher (or restart the phone) and open Home: the contacts line or your contacts still show correctly, and the dock is still in place. Put the date back to automatic.
