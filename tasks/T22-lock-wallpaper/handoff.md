# Handoff: T22-lock-wallpaper

What changed: page renamed "Lock screen and always-on display" (row and title; the help text keeps the old name). New card on top of `MedievalAod.kt`: preview, button "Set the orb as my lock screen", button "Put my old wallpaper back" (only if a backup exists), result line. Logic in new `MedievalLockWallpaper.kt`: `WallpaperManager.setBitmap(bitmap, null, true, FLAG_LOCK)` (lock only, never home); result 0, SecurityException and others give a plain line and a `medieval` log. Before the first change the old lock wallpaper is copied via `getWallpaperFile(FLAG_LOCK)` into `filesDir/lock-backup.bin` (null result = no separate lock wallpaper, marker `lock-backup.none`, restore then calls `clear(FLAG_LOCK)`); if reading fails the set still happens and the line says the old one cannot be put back. Manifest: `SET_WALLPAPER` added (it was not declared; own commit, upstream file).
Clock-safe picture: `assets/src/lib.js` `wpC(scale, cy)` and `render.html#wall-lock` (orb scaled 0.68, centred at 70 percent of the height, spans about 59 to 80 percent). Output `assets/wallpaper/deliverance-orb-lock-1080x2400.png` (4 MB) and the shipped JPEG q93 `app/ui/src/main/assets/lock/deliverance-orb-lock.jpg` (0.8 MB APK growth). Mock `docs/design/T22-lock-mock.png` (stock layout drawn from the owner's screenshot): music card plus 2 cards cover about half the ring, plus 4 cards nearly all of it.
Trade-off: a bigger orb would reach up under the clock and icon row; at this size it is clear of them and of the bottom shortcuts, but any notification list longer than the free space (about 54 to 90 percent when the music card is shown, from about 44 percent when not) hides it. That is the nature of a lock screen; no picture avoids it with a visible orb.
Pictures: `docs/design/shots/T22/t22-page-gold.png`, `t22-page-ember.png` (looked at Ember). Test `T21Shots.kt` now writes to `shots/T22` and passes the new params. Rename of the title also in `MedievalAod.kt` only.
NOT verified: Samsung's handling of FLAG_LOCK (it may ignore or replace it); whether `getWallpaperFile(FLAG_LOCK)` is allowed without a storage permission on Android 13 (SecurityException is caught and shown); persistence after restart; where the stock clock really sits (mock is drawn from one screenshot); the 5-step list still says "5 steps" while the testlist uses the shorter 6-tap version; LockStar steps are generic (S8 testlist not in this branch).
