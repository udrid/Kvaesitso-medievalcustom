# Phone checks for T22: lock screen picture (short version)

## 1. Install
1. Install the new build from GitHub (the same way as before).
2. Open Deliverance once so it starts.

## 2. Put the orb on the lock screen
1. On Home, tap the weekday name to open Settings, then tap "Lock screen and always-on display".
2. Tap "Set the orb as my lock screen". You should see the line "Done. Lock the phone to see it."
3. Press the side button to lock the phone, then wake it. You should see the dark ground with the small gold ring and the orb low on the screen. The clock and date stay clear of it. Report if the line shows an error instead, and what it says.

## 3. The always-on picture (optional, 6 taps in Samsung's own menu)
1. On the same page tap "Save all eight". You should see "Saved to Pictures/Deliverance".
2. Tap "Open Always On Display settings", then tap Lock screen, then Always On Display.
3. Tap Clock style, then Image clock, then Gallery. Pick a picture from the album Deliverance (the plain ring one works every day). Tap Done.
4. Colour: if Samsung shows a colour dot for the clock, pick the cream-yellow one.

## 4. Only if the clock overlaps the ring (LockStar, optional)
1. Open LockStar, then Lock screen. Look at the preview.
2. If the orb is behind the clock or the four round icons, use the clock position setting to move the clock up a little. Do not touch the other settings.
3. Report with a screenshot if you are unsure.

## 5. Undo
1. Open the same page and tap "Put my old wallpaper back". You should see "Your old lock screen picture is back." (The button only shows if the app could save your old one.)
2. If it does not work: Samsung Settings, Wallpaper and style, Change wallpapers, choose your own picture for the lock screen.

## What to report
- The line shown under the lock-screen button (Done, or the error).
- A photo of the lock screen with 0 and with 2 or more notifications.
- Whether the picture is still there after you restart the phone (hold the side button, Restart) and after you restart Deliverance.

## Should NOT happen
- The Home screen of Deliverance changes its background.
- A permission pop-up asking for photos or files.
- The orb sits under the clock or the four round icons.
- Your old lock picture is gone and the undo button is missing without any explanation.
