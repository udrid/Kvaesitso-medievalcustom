# T18 testlist: starred contacts light up (owner steps)

Before you start: in the Contacts app, give one person a star (open the person, tap the star). Deliverance needs Contacts and Notification access (the "Dispatches" panel on Home already asks for the second one).

1. Ask a friend to send that starred person's number a text, or to send you a WhatsApp/Messages text from that person. Leave the message unread (do not open it).
2. Go to the Home screen of Deliverance and tap the command bar ("Command the Oracle") so the typing layer opens.
   - You should see the "People and switches" panel with the starred person's round tile.
   - On that tile there should be a small red badge with a gold rim and the number of unread messages (1, 2, ...).
3. Keep the typing layer open and ask for one more message.
   - The badge number goes up and the badge swells once; a soft gold glow rings the tile for about 2 seconds, once.
4. Ask the same person (or someone with a starred number) to ring you and do not answer.
   - You should see the count go up by one for that person.
5. Tap the tile with the badge.
   - The message app (or phone app) should open on that conversation. Then come back: the contact card was NOT opened.
6. Swipe the notification away in the notification shade (or read the message).
   - The badge must be gone when you open the typing layer again.
7. Restart: swipe Deliverance away from the recent apps, open it again, open the typing layer.
   - Messages that are still waiting in the shade show their badge again; dismissed ones stay gone.

Should NOT happen:
- A badge on a contact who is NOT starred (they never appear in the panel).
- A badge staying after the notification was dismissed.
- Any glow or swell with Battery Saver on (Settings > Battery > Power saving). With it on, a badge still shows, but still, no motion.
- A badge on a tile for an e-mail, a calendar reminder or an app update from that person.
- A tile with no badge: tap should still open the contact card as before.

Report: which apps you tested (Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, the phone app) and for each whether the badge showed. If one does not, tell me which one.
