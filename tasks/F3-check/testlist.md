# Phone test for the build with contact alerts, live colours and Corner style
1. Contact alerts. Open the phone's Contacts app, open a person you often message and tap the star. Leave the launcher. Ask that person to text you (or send one from another phone).
   Open the launcher, tap "Command the Oracle". You should see that person's round picture with a small red number, and a short glow once. Tap it: their chat opens.
2. Clear it. Swipe the text away in the notification shade. Back in the launcher the red number is gone.
3. Colours at once. On Home press and hold the weekday letter to open Settings, choose the colour scheme "Royal Blue", press Back until you see Home. Do NOT turn the phone.
   You should see the big letter, weekday, date, round planet badge, divider and weather picture, the panels, shields and the bottom seals all blue at the same moment. Repeat with Ember and Bone, then back to Gold.
4. Corner style. In Settings tap "Appearance". You should see only "Transparency" and "Corner style". Tap "Corner style", choose "Round", go Home, open "Command the Oracle".
   Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the other switches are now round pills. Go back, choose "Sharp": they turn square without closing the launcher.
5. Buttons. Long-press an app and choose hide: "Cancel" and "Hide" are round in Round and square in Sharp.
6. Restart. Choose "Round" and a colour you like, swipe the launcher away in recent apps, open it again. Round and your colour are still there.
Should NOT happen: gold left in the header after choosing a blue scheme; panels, ribbons or frames turning round; the round wax-seal switches turning square; a red number on someone you did NOT star, or on a group chat where a different person wrote last; any change needing a turn of the phone or a restart.
Report: for each step what you saw; for step 1 the app you were texted in (Samsung Messages, WhatsApp ...) and whether a MISSED CALL from a starred person also shows a number.
