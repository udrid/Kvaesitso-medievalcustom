# Phone test: S5 gestures and the apps page

In one line: swiping down does nothing any more, and swiping up opens a page with a search bar, your favourites, your apps sorted into categories, and a "Today" strip.

Things I chose for you (say if you want them different): one category opens at a time; the bar on this page filters apps as you type; the bar says "Search your apps..."; swipe left, swipe right, double tap and long press are set to do nothing ONCE at the first start (you can change them again in Settings > Gestures); Home button unchanged.

1. Swipe up the page
   - Where: on the Home screen.
   - Do: swipe up from the middle of the screen.
   - You should see: a framed search bar, then a "Favourites" or "Most used" panel (only if you have used apps through Deliverance), then "The Armoury" with category tiles (Communication, Social, Media and Music, Games, Tools, ...) with a number on each, then a "Today" strip with your next calendar event and the weather. Your old widgets, if any, are below.
2. Open a category
   - Do: tap "Tools". Then tap "Social".
   - You should see: Tools opens and shows its apps as little gold shields with names. Tapping Social closes Tools and opens Social. Tapping an app opens it.
3. Search
   - Do: tap the search bar, type two letters of an app name.
   - You should see: one panel "Found" with the matching apps. Clear the text: the categories come back.
4. Move an app
   - Do: open "Other" (if it has apps). Press and HOLD one app until a menu shows. Tap "Move to category...", pick one.
   - You should see: the app leaves "Other" and shows in the category you picked. "Add to favourites" puts it in the top panel.
5. Restart the launcher
   - Do: swipe up, remove Deliverance from recent apps, open it again, swipe up.
   - You should see: your moved apps and favourites are still where you put them.
6. Swipe down (should NOT happen)
   - Do: swipe down from the middle of Home, several times. Also press the Home button on Home.
   - You should NOT see a blank search page. The "Swipe down" row is gone from Settings > Gestures. The command bar on Home still opens search with the keyboard.
7. Other gestures (should NOT happen)
   - Do: swipe left, swipe right, double tap and long press on empty space.
   - You should see nothing happen (double tap used to lock the screen; that was set to nothing once). You can pick them again in Settings > Gestures.
8. Palette and corners
   - Do: Settings > pick Ember palette (and the Corner style setting), come back, swipe up.
   - You should see the page in the new colours straight away.
9. What to report: which categories look wrong (name the app and the category you expected), whether "Today" shows your event and weather, and whether swiping up feels smooth.
