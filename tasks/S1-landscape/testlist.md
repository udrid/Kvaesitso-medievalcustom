# S1-landscape: what to check on the phone
Owner picks used: layout A (three columns), standing pill, dock as a row under the orb.

## Before you start (once)
1. Swipe down from the top of the phone and make sure "Auto rotate" is ON (the Auto-rotate chip on the Deliverance typing layer shows it too).
2. In Deliverance open Settings, Home screen, and find "Fixed screen rotation". It must be OFF. Go back to Home.

## A. Rotate and watch (the main check)
1. Hold the phone upright on Home. Turn it on its side (camera on the left, then camera on the right).
2. You should see Home re-arrange in about a third of a second: header, Treasury and the four flags on the left; the orb with its four shields in the middle and the four wax seals under it; Ledger, command bar and Dispatches on the right. Each part slides to its new place. The orb stays in the middle.
3. Turn the phone back upright: everything slides back to the portrait layout, looking exactly like before.
4. Report: does it look smooth or does it jump or flash? Roughly how long does it take? Do you see a flash of the old picture turning (that would be Samsung's own animation)?
5. Should NOT happen: the launcher restarting (blank or black flash, the clock jumping), parts flickering, anything staying half-way.

## B. Everything works in landscape
1. Look at the black pill around the camera hole. It should stand upright along the edge with the hole in it: notification icons above the hole, Hushed, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and signal below. No part of Home may touch it.
2. Tap the orb: the Claude app opens. Come back. Tap each of the four shields: the right app opens (or the picker if empty). Long-press a shield: the picker opens.
3. Tap each wax seal (Herald, Portrait, Road, Smithy). Each opens its app.
4. Look at the four flags (battery, storage, memory, heat): numbers show and the gold level looks right.
5. Ledger and Dispatches: text readable, long lines end in "..." instead of running over something. Swipe a Dispatches line sideways: it goes away, as in portrait.
6. Long-press the header: Deliverance settings open.
7. Should NOT happen: any part overlapping another, a button too close to the navigation buttons to press, a part drawn under the camera pill, text too small to read, the weekday name touching the small round sign next to it.

## C. Typing layer and keyboard in landscape
1. In landscape tap the command bar (right column). The typing layer opens and the keyboard comes up.
2. You should see the text field on top, "Most used" apps on the left and the contacts and switches on the right, side by side. Slide them up and down if the keyboard hides part.
3. Type "wifi" and press nothing else. Rotate the phone upright and back to landscape.
4. Should NOT happen: the typed text disappearing, the typing layer closing by itself when you rotate, the field hidden behind the camera or the navigation buttons.
5. Press the Home button: the layer closes.

## D. Animations setting
1. Open Deliverance settings (long-press the header), set Animations to Off, go back to Home and rotate. The parts must jump into place at once, no sliding.
2. Set Animations back to Full or Minimal and rotate: the slide is back.

## E. Restart check
1. Rotate to landscape, then close Deliverance from the recent-apps list (swipe it away) and open it again by pressing Home.
2. It must open straight in the layout the phone is in now, with no slide, and the shield choices and Treasury text unchanged.
