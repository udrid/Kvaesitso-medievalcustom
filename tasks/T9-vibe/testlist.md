# T9-vibe: checks on the phone

Install the new Deliverance build first (the coordinator sends the steps). Then go to the Home screen.

1. **The new orb**
   - Look at the orb in the middle.
   - You should see: a glass ball of dark red smoke full of tiny gold specks, a soft light patch up-left, a small bright heart, set in a gold ring with four red gems.
   - Report: does it look like the picture you picked (2a-3, heavy gold dust)?

2. **The ring writing**
   - Look at the Latin words around the orb.
   - You should see: dark letters that look cut into bronze, with a thin light edge under each letter (no bright gold).

3. **The orb moves slowly**
   - Watch the orb for about 20 seconds.
   - You should see: the smoke turns very slowly and a few gold specks twinkle. It should be calm, never fast or flashing.

4. **Tap reactions**
   - Tap a shield, then press Back to return.
   - You should see: the shield flashes gold and swells a tiny bit for a moment before the app opens.
   - Do the same with a wax seal at the bottom (it presses in like a stamp) and the orb (gold flash).

5. **The orb wakes up**
   - Press the power button to turn the screen off. Wait 5 seconds, then turn it on and unlock.
   - You should see: the orb starts dark and its heart flares up to full glow in about one second.

6. **Charging pulse**
   - Plug in the charger and stay on Home.
   - You should see: the orb's heart and glow slowly swell and fade, about every 4 to 5 seconds.
   - Unplug: the pulsing stops.

7. **Notification glow**
   - Ask someone to message you (or send yourself a message in an app on a shield) while Home is on screen.
   - You should see: the Dispatches panel glows gold once, and the red number badge on that app's shield swells twice.

8. **Ledger: an event is near**
   - In your calendar, make an event that starts in 10 minutes. Go back to Home and wait up to 30 seconds.
   - You should see: the red cross next to that event in Today's Ledger pulses twice, then stops.

9. **The Animations setting** (Claude proposed what each choice means; you can change it)
   - Long-press the date/day at the top of Home to open the settings. At the top there is a new "Animations" line.
   - Tap it and choose "Full", then press Back to Home.
   - You should see: about every 20 seconds a faint warm light walks once round the ring writing.

10. **Restart keeps the setting**
    - Restart the phone (or force-stop Deliverance in Android settings > Apps).
    - Open Home and the settings again: "Animations" should still show what you chose.

11. **Turn it all off**
    - In settings choose "Off: nothing moves", then go back to Home.
    - You should see: the orb is still, no twinkle, no ring light, taps open apps with no flash.
    - Set it back to "Minimal" (or your choice) afterwards.

12. **Battery Saver**
    - Turn on Battery Saver (pull down the quick panel, tap Power saving).
    - Go to Home: nothing should move at all (orb still, no twinkle, no pulse, no ring light).
    - Turn Battery Saver off: the orb moves again within a second.

## Should NOT happen
- Nothing moves fast, flashes repeatedly or draws your eye while you just look at Home.
- With "Off" or Battery Saver, nothing moves at all (see steps 11 and 12).
- The ring light never shows in "Minimal" (only in "Full").
- The orb does NOT flare up every time you come back from an app, only after the screen was off.
- The Ledger cross does NOT keep pulsing: once per event.
- No crash, no white or empty circle where the orb is.

## Battery (after 2 or 3 days of normal use)
- Android settings > Battery > Battery usage: find Deliverance and report its percentage.

## Not changed in this stage
- The red day letter's "new day" shimmer (you picked it) is not built yet: its file is being changed by another task (T10). It comes in a follow-up.
- The small weather picture under the clock still moves as before and does not follow the new Animations setting yet (same reason).
