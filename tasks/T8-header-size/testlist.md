# Test list: T8-header-size (header a bit bigger)

1. Install the new APK and press Home. Look at the top right of the header.
   You should see: the time, the weather line and the little weather picture are a bit bigger than before (about one seventh).
   Report: is it now big enough, too big, or still small?
2. Look at the gold line under the header.
   You should see: the weather picture sits fully above the line with a small gap. It does not touch or cross it.
   Report: gap yes/no.
3. Read the weather text under the time (for example "14° · Partly cloudy").
   You should see: the whole text on one line.
   Report: is the end cut off with "..."? Tell me what it said.
4. Check the day name, the round sign (medallion) and the time.
   Report: do any of them touch or overlap each other? On Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday days, especially.
5. Check that the little camera pill, the sections below and the dock are exactly where they were.
6. Should NOT happen: picture touching the line, any text cut off, anything jumping around or moved.
7. Close the launcher fully (swipe it away in recent apps), open it again.
   Report: the sizes and the gap are the same as before closing.
