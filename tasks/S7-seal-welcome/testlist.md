# Phone checks for S7: the welcome after you unlock

Before you start: open the launcher's Settings (tap the weekday on Home). Near the top, under "Animations", you should see "Welcome animation" set to "On". If it says Off, set it to On. Also make sure Battery Saver is off and the battery is above 15 %.

1. Fingerprint unlock. Stay on the Home screen, press the power button, wait 5 seconds, unlock with your fingerprint.
   You should see, for about one and a half seconds: a thin gold circle drawn round the glass orb with a little spark running at its tip, the four wax seals at the bottom cracking one after another and falling away to show their pictures, the Home panels fading up one by one from the top, and the orb waking from dark to glowing as the circle closes. Then Home looks exactly as always. Report: did it play, was it too slow or too busy?
2. Swipe or PIN unlock. Lock the phone again, wait 5 seconds, unlock with a swipe, PIN or pattern instead.
   You should see the same show. Report if it did not play, or played while you were still typing the PIN.
3. Touch during the show. Lock, unlock, and tap anywhere on Home while the animation runs.
   You should see everything jump to the normal Home at once, and the tap should work (the orb opens Claude, a seal opens its app). Nothing may stay half-faded.
4. Should NOT happen: lock the phone while you are INSIDE another app (not Home), then unlock. You come back to that app, and no welcome plays. Then press the Home button: Home just appears, no show.
5. Should NOT happen: with Home showing and the phone unlocked, press the Home button or open and leave an app. No show. The show is only for an unlock.
6. Settings check. In Settings set "Welcome animation" to "Off", lock, wait, unlock: Home just appears (the orb still wakes up gently as before). Turn it back On.
7. Restart check. Choose "Off", swipe the launcher away from recent apps, open it again: the setting should still say Off. Turn it On again.
8. Colours. In Settings change "Colour scheme" to Ember, then repeat step 1. The gold line, sparks and seals should be in Ember colours.
9. Battery Saver. Switch Battery Saver on, lock, unlock: no show, Home just appears. Switch it off again.

What to report: which steps worked, and for step 1 and 2 how it felt (too long, too short, too much, just right). If a show ever left the screen half empty or dark, tell me how you unlocked.
