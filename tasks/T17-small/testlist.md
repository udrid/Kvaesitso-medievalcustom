# T17-small: what to check on the phone

## A. The weather line fits whole
1. Open Deliverance (press Home). Look at the small weather text under the clock, top right.
2. You should see the whole line, for example "14° · Partly cloudy", no "..." at the end. The temperature must always be there. It may look a little smaller than before on long words.
3. Report: the exact text you see, and whether the clock, the little picture under it and the line under the header look unmoved.
4. Should NOT happen: letters cut off with "...", the picture under the weather moving up or down, the clock changing size.

## B. The red first letter shimmers on a new day
1. On the phone open Settings, General management, Date and time. Turn off "Automatic date and time" and set the date to TOMORROW.
2. Press the Home button (the launcher must come to the front). You should see one soft gold gleam sweep across the big red first letter of the weekday (about 1.5 seconds).
3. Press Home again, open an app and come back several times. The gleam must NOT play again today.
4. Now put the date back: turn "Automatic date and time" on again. Press Home. Nothing should shimmer (a gleam on "going back" is fine only once, if the date changed; report it).
5. Should NOT happen: a gleam on every Home press, a gleam with Battery Saver on (turn Battery Saver on, set the date to the day after tomorrow, press Home: no gleam, and none later after turning Battery Saver off), the weekday name or the gold outline of the letter changing.

## C. The clock keeps going
1. Leave Home on screen for 3 minutes. The minutes on the clock must advance by themselves (it may change a second or two after the full minute).
2. Lock the phone for a minute, wake it. The clock must already show the right time.
3. Open an app, wait 2 minutes, press Home: the clock and the three small flags (battery, storage, memory) must show current values at once.
4. Should NOT happen: a frozen clock, the old time for a moment after waking, anything moved.
