# S6-landscape-fill: what to check on the phone
Owner pick used: option D (taller side columns and a six-hour Forecast strip between the shields and the dock).
Landscape Home only. Portrait must look exactly as before.

## A. The new landscape Home (main check)
1. Open Deliverance on Home and turn the phone on its side (camera on the left). Wait a second.
2. You should see: on the left the header, a bigger Treasury panel and four taller flags; in the middle the orb (same size as before) with its four shields, and under them a new small panel called FORECAST with six hours (time, a small picture, a temperature); the four wax seals under that; on the right Today's Ledger with room for four events, the command bar, and Dispatches with room for five lines. The empty space at the bottom of both side columns should be mostly gone.
3. Turn the phone the other way (camera on the right). Same layout, only the sides swap.
4. Report: does anything overlap (shield names "MESSAGES" and "DISCORD" against the right column, the Forecast panel against the shields or the seals)? Is any text cut off or too small to read?
5. Should NOT happen: the orb getting smaller, anything touching the camera pill or the button bar, parts flickering.

## B. Forecast strip
1. The six temperatures should match your phone's weather for the coming hours (same weather as the header). Times show as 3 PM style or 15:00 style like your phone clock.
2. If the weather is not set up or has no data yet, the panel says so in plain words (for example "Fetching forecast…") instead of showing numbers. Report what you saw.
3. Should NOT happen: made-up numbers, or the strip moving or blinking.

## C. Ledger, Dispatches, Treasury
1. Ledger shows up to four events (fewer if your calendar has fewer; then the panel stays the same height). Dispatches shows up to five lines; swipe one sideways to dismiss it as before.
2. Treasury looks larger and its text does not run into the "of the way" line.

## D. Portrait and turning
1. Turn the phone upright: Home looks exactly like before this change (Ledger two events, Dispatches three lines, no Forecast panel).
2. Turn it on its side and back a few times: parts slide as before; the Forecast panel fades in and out.

## E. Colours
1. Settings, pick another colour scheme (for example Ember), go back to Home in landscape: the Forecast panel, taller flags and the bigger panels all take the new colours at once.

## F. Restart
1. Nothing in this change saves data. Still, swipe the launcher away from the recent apps and open it again in landscape: the same layout comes back.

## G. Command bar and long titles (added by the coordinator)
1. On landscape Home, look at the bar with the quill: it should read "Command the Oracle…" in full, with a clear gap before the SUMMON button. Check both turns (camera left, then right).
2. Report if the words touch or run under SUMMON.
3. In Today's Ledger, a long event title ends in "…" inside the panel and never runs past its edge or under the next row.
