# Phone test: T11-command (Home command bar)

1. Go to the Home screen. Tap the bar "Command the Oracle…".
   You should see: the search page opens, the keyboard comes up by itself and a blinking cursor sits in the search box.
   You should NOT see: a long list of all your apps, or the keyboard missing (you having to tap again).
2. Type `cam`. You should see Camera (and other matches) appear under the box.
3. Clear the box. Type a short question, for example `What is a trebuchet?`. Tap SUMMON in the search box.
   You should see: the Claude app opens with exactly that question in the message box (or paste it; it is also copied). Tap Send yourself.
   You should NOT see: an older question from before.
4. Press Back (swipe back). The search closes and Home returns. Tap the bar again: the box must be empty and again no app list.
5. On Home, tap SUMMON on the bar without typing. You should see the search open with the keyboard and a short note "Write thy question first."
6. Restart the launcher: press the Home button, then swipe Deliverance away from recent apps and open it again. Repeat step 1. Nothing is saved by this feature, it must behave the same.

Report: what you saw in steps 1, 3 and 5. A photo of step 1 helps.
