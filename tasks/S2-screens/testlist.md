# Phone test: S2-screens (the rest of the launcher in the Deliverance look)

In one line: every page of the launcher except Home now has the dark gold look: sharp corners, framed panels with red ribbons, old-style letters, and red wax-seal switches.

1. Search results
   - On Home tap the bar, type "mar" (or any app name).
   - You should see: each group (Apps, Contacts, Calendar...) as one panel with a double gold line and a red ribbon on its top edge. The ribbon must sit ON TOP of the gold lines: no gold line may cross the ribbon.
   - Report: yes/no, and which group looks wrong (names run into the ribbon, lines cut off, gaps).
2. Open a result
   - Tap a contact or a calendar entry. It grows into a framed card with sharp corners.
   - You should see: gold text on dark, no purple, no round corners.
3. Settings
   - Open Deliverance settings (long-press an empty spot, or the usual way). Open Search, then Appearance.
   - You should see: the title in old-style letters, each group in a framed panel with a red ribbon, grain and a dark edge like Home, red wax seals instead of switches.
   - Tap a seal: it slides right and turns red with a gold ring. Tap again: slides back and goes dark.
   - Report: yes/no, anything still purple or round.
4. Dialogs and sheets
   - In a settings page open something that asks a question (for example hide an item, or the Animations list). Also open the add-widget sheet from the widget page.
   - You should see: a framed dialog with a red ribbon reading DELIVERANCE; sheets rise with a small red pennant at the top instead of the grey bar.
5. Should NOT happen
   - No screen may crash, flash white, or show text you cannot read (dark text on dark).
   - Home itself must look exactly as before.
   - Sliding the seal must not move the page or open anything.
6. Restart
   - Change one switch in settings, swipe Deliverance away from Recents, reopen it, open the same settings page: the seal must be where you left it.
7. Please also report (not checked by me)
   - Does scrolling a long settings page or the results feel as smooth as before?
   - Is the small cream text easy to read (grain over text)?
