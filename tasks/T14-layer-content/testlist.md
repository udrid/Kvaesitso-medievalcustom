# Phone test: T14-layer-content (what the typing layer shows before you type)

What changed in one line: tap "Command the Oracle..." on Home. The page that opens now has three calm parts under the bar: your most used apps, your starred contacts, and quick switches.

1. See the layer
   - Be on Home. Tap the bar. The keyboard opens.
   - You should see: under the bar, a red ribbon "MOST USED" with up to 8 shields (2 rows of 4, name beneath), then a framed panel "PEOPLE AND SWITCHES" with starred contacts (round initials or photos) and 7 pill-shaped switches. Scroll up/down if the keyboard hides the lower part.
   - Report: yes/no, and anything that looks crowded or wrong.
2. Most used apps
   - Open two or three apps from the Home shields or from this layer (e.g. Chrome twice), then come back and tap the bar.
   - You should see: those apps first. A fresh install may show few or none (the section hides itself when empty).
3. People
   - You should see your STARRED contacts (star them in the Contacts app if none show; the section hides if none or if Contacts permission is off). Tap one: the contact card opens.
4. The switches (what each does)
   - Flashlight: tap. The phone's light turns on and the chip shows a filled gold dot. Tap again: off. REAL toggle.
   - Sound: tap. The chip changes to "Vibrate" (phone vibrates mode). Tap again: back to "Sound". If you have already allowed Do Not Disturb access for Deliverance, a third state "Mute" appears. REAL toggle (UNVERIFIED on this phone).
   - Auto-rotate: first tap shows a hint and opens "Modify system settings" for Deliverance. Switch it on, press Back, tap the chip again: it toggles auto-rotate, dot filled = on. REAL toggle after that one-time permission.
   - Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Power saving, Location: tap opens the phone's own panel or page. The chip is brighter gold when it is currently ON. Android 13 does not let apps flip these.
   - Report: which of these did what you expected.
5. Should NOT happen
   - The sections must disappear the moment you type a letter (only search results show); clearing the text brings them back.
   - Flashlight/Sound must not open any page or ask for permission. Nothing should crash.
6. Restart
   - The most-used order is saved. Swipe Deliverance away from Recents, reopen it, tap the bar: same apps are listed.
