# Testlist T12-orb-tune (owner, on the phone)

Install the new APK (after the build is green and you said yes to the push). Go to Home.

## 1. The Oracle label is gone
1. Look at the orb in the middle of the Home screen.
2. You should see the orb and its ring, and EMPTY dark space where "The Oracle" used to be.
3. Report: is anything else moved (Treasury, banners, shields, Ledger, command bar, dock)? It should NOT be.

## 2. The orb turns slowly
1. Look at the orb for a full minute (a watch or the clock on Home helps). Look at one gold dust grain.
2. You should see the smoke turn only a little: about 5 degrees in 6 seconds, so after one minute it has turned about as far as one minute-hand tick (6 degrees) at most. Gold dust twinkles softly on and off (about every 5 seconds). It should feel calm and alive, not jumpy.
3. Report: too slow, too fast, or good? Does the twinkle look choppy or smooth?
4. Should NOT happen: the orb spinning visibly fast, or flickering.

## 3. See each tap reaction (press and HOLD, do not release yet)
1. Press and HOLD one wax seal at the bottom (Herald, Portrait, Road, Smithy) with a finger.
2. You should see it sink in a little and go a bit darker at once, and stay that way while you hold.
3. Slide your finger away and let go: it should fade back and the app should NOT open.
4. Do the same on one shield (X, Discord, YouTube, Messages): hold; it swells slightly and flashes gold.
5. Do the same on the orb: hold; it swells slightly and flashes gold.
6. Now just TAP quickly on a seal, a shield and the orb. You should see a quick flash or press, and the app opens about a blink (0.12 second) later.
7. Report: could you see the flash/press on a quick tap? Did all apps still open?
8. Should NOT happen: tapping twice quickly opening the app twice; the app opening before you see anything.

## 4. Animations Off opens instantly
1. Open Deliverance settings (long press on the date, or the launcher settings) and set Animations to Off.
2. Go back to Home and tap a seal, a shield and the orb.
3. You should see NO flash and NO press, and the app opens with no wait.
4. Should NOT happen: any delay before the app opens (more than a blink). Same with Battery Saver turned on.
5. Set Animations back to Minimal when done.

## 5. Command bar unchanged
1. Tap "Command the Oracle..." on Home.
2. The keyboard opens right away as before (no extra wait).

## 6. After a restart of the launcher
1. Swipe Deliverance away from recent apps, open it again.
2. The orb still turns slowly and the label is still gone; the Animations setting is still what you set.

## 7. Battery check after a day (optional, no tool needed)
1. Use the phone normally for a day with Deliverance as your launcher.
2. Open Settings, then Battery (Battery and device care), then Battery usage.
3. Pick "Since last full charge" or "Today", find "Deliverance" and write down its percentage and the screen-on time.
4. Report both numbers. Expect it to be small (about 1 % or less) next to the screen itself. For a comparison, set Animations to Off for the next day and compare.

## 8. Weather picture freezes when saving power
1. On Home, look at the small weather picture under the clock. You should see it move in a choppy, hand-drawn way (about 5 steps a second).
2. Turn Battery Saver on (swipe down the quick panel, tap Power saving). Return to Home.
3. You should see the weather picture stand still (one calm frame, no lightning stuck on it) and the orb smoke and dust stop moving.
4. Turn Battery Saver off: both start moving again. Setting Animations to Off should freeze them the same way.
5. Should NOT happen: the weather picture still moving with Battery Saver on.
