# R1 landscape fix: what to check on the phone
Compare with the PC pictures in `docs/design/shots/` (home-landscape-camera-left.png and home-landscape-camera-right.png).

## A. Turn the phone (the main check)
1. On Home, make sure Auto rotate is on (swipe down from the top, the "Auto rotate" tile is lit).
2. Turn the phone on its side with the camera on the LEFT. You should see three columns filling the whole screen from top to bottom: on the left the weekday, date, clock, weather, Treasury and the four flags; in the middle the orb with four shields and, at the bottom, the four red wax seals with their names; on the right "Today's Ledger", the command bar, and "Dispatches".
3. Turn it so the camera is on the RIGHT. You should see the same layout, moved away from the navigation buttons (now on the left).
4. Turn it upright again. Home should look exactly as before this fix.

## B. Look closely in landscape
1. Top edge: "DAY OF ..." and the red "TODAY'S LEDGER" ribbon are fully visible, not cut.
2. Middle: the wax seals sit clearly below the lower shields and their names; no line crosses the orb's ring.
3. Right column, top to bottom: Ledger with two rows, then the command bar on its own, then the red "DISPATCHES" ribbon and its contents. A long event name ends in "..." inside the panel.
4. Left column: the little weather picture (sun, clouds, castle) sits above the gold divider line, not on it.

## C. Should NOT happen
1. Anything overlapping anything else, or drawn under the camera pill or the navigation buttons.
2. A large empty band at the bottom or top of the screen.
3. The stage shrinking or jumping when you swipe Home up or down to open the search or the app list and come back.
4. Portrait looking different from before.

## D. Typing layer
1. In landscape tap the command bar. You should see the text field on top and the two panes (most used apps, contacts and switches) side by side. Press Home to close it.

## Report
Tell the coordinator A to D pass or fail, with a screenshot of each landscape turn. If the top is still cut, also tell us; the next thread will read a line the app writes ("Stage: box ...").
