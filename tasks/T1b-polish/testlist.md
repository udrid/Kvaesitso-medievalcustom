# Phone checks: T1b-polish

Install the new APK from the GitHub Actions run for branch `claude/T1b-polish` (same way as last time). Smart Launcher stays installed.

1. Look at the top of the Home screen.
   - Be on the Home screen after opening Deliverance.
   - Look at the row under the date and clock.
   - You should see only "Bluetooth" and "Hushed" on the left. On the right, under the big clock, you should see the weather line (or `[weather]` if no weather is set up). Nothing should overlap.
   - Report: is it less cluttered, and does anything touch or overlap?
2. Should NOT happen: "Wi-Fi call" and "Voicemail" must be gone, and no text may sit on top of other text anywhere in the top rows.
3. Look at the Treasury box.
   - Look at `[YOUR TOTAL]` and the Quest bar to its right.
   - You should see a gap between them; the words must not touch the bar.
   - Report: does the total look too small?
4. Open the app picker.
   - Tap a shield (or press and hold a shield that already has an app).
   - You should see a dark panel with a search bar on top and a grid of apps: icon above name, 4 per row, alphabetical. Scroll up and down.
   - Report: do all the names fit, and are any apps missing?
5. Search.
   - Tap the search bar and type 2 or 3 letters of an app name (for example "cam").
   - The grid should shrink to apps whose name contains those letters.
   - Clear the letters: the whole list returns.
6. Replace an app.
   - Tap an app in the grid.
   - The panel closes and that shield shows the app you picked. Tap the shield: the app should open.
7. Should NOT happen: tapping outside the panel should just close it and change nothing.
8. Restart check.
   - Swipe up from the recent-apps button (or open Recents) and close Deliverance. Press the Home button.
   - Your picked app should still be on the shield.
9. Look at the ring around the glowing orb.
   - You should see three phrases, evenly spread around the circle: NOSCE TE IPSUM at the top, SAPERE AUDE lower right, LUX ET VERITAS lower left, with a small cross between each pair.
   - The letters should be evenly spaced, sit in the middle of the band between the two thin gold rings, and touch neither ring.
   - Every phrase should read the right way up (no phrase upside-down). At the bottom the letters face up, at the top they also face up.
   - Report: any phrase that is cut off, overlaps a cross, or touches a ring line.
